Research

Our research interests are applications of chemometrics in clinical metabolomics and the development of wearables for disease diagnostics. The group is currently working on rapid diagnosis of tuberculosis.

Wearable sampling

Non-invasive wearable sampling devices for disease diagnostics.

Smell-led metabolomics

Metabolomics analysis pipelines led by the volatile molecules that carry a smell.

Chemometrics tools

Tools for biomarker discovery in clinical metabolomics data.

We collaborate with cross-continental clinical research teams in countries such as the USA, in Africa and in India. Within the University of Manchester, the group collaborates on the diagnosis of Parkinson's disease and on creating a chemometrics hub, statistics for all, within the Manchester Institute of Biotechnology.

Software

Tattvā

A desktop toolkit for LC-MS and GC-MS metabolomics. It runs on your own computer, so your data stay there. Versions are available for macOS, Windows and Linux. A licence file from the Trivedi Group is needed before it will run.

People

Dr Drupad Trivedi

Drupad received his BSc (Hons) in Biomedical Sciences and his PhD in biomarker discovery using metabolomics from Middlesex University. He briefly trained as a postdoctoral fellow at the London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine, then worked at the University of Manchester with Prof Royston Goodacre and Prof Perdita Barran before starting independent research at the University of Manchester.
